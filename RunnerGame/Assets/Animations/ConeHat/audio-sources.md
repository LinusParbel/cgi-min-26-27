# Cone intro audio sources

Runtime audio is mono PCM16 at 22,050 Hz. Recorded effects are public-domain
CC0 Freesound previews, normalized and converted for the game:

| Runtime file | Source and use | Processing |
|---|---|---|
| `step.wav`, `walk_short.wav`, `mount_step.wav` | [FOSSarts, “walking indoors on concrete floor 1”](https://freesound.org/people/FOSSarts/sounds/740419/), raw adult male footsteps on concrete, CC0 | Mono, 22,050 Hz, normalized; full three-second walk, two-second cut and short shoe contact. |
| `run.wav` | [Mihacappy, “cretestepping.wav”](https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/817387/), half-sprinting on concrete in work shoes, CC0 | First 4.1 seconds, mono, 22,050 Hz, normalized. |
| `kickstand.wav` | [RoofDog, “Kickstand.wav”](https://freesound.org/people/RoofDog/sounds/78978/), bicycle kickstand clicking down/up, CC0 | First 0.85 seconds, mono, 22,050 Hz, normalized for the scooter mounting beat. |

`hey.wav` and `stop.wav` are cut from one locally synthesized German recording
made with the installed Microsoft Hedda Desktop voice: “Hey!” and “Stehen
bleiben!” are split at the quiet gap in `call.wav`. `stop_firm.wav` and
`stop_angry.wav` are progressively faster and louder processed versions of
`stop.wav`, used as the officer loses patience.

| Runtime file | Source and use | Processing |
|---|---|---|
| `pursuit.wav` | [balloonhead, “03 there he is, after him.wav”](https://freesound.org/people/balloonhead/sounds/443433/), human-recorded German military commands, CC0; used as the officer's distant chase shout when the rider runs. | Converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 and loudness-normalized. |

The chase clip replaces another repeat of the synthetic “Hey!” during the run.
The synthetic calls still need listening review in the full mix; source and
license details for the recorded clip are on the linked Freesound page.

`handling.wav`, `impact.wav`, `beep.wav`, `takeoff.wav`, and `ambience.wav` are
project-authored effects generated by the ignored local
`Art/assets/cone_hat/generate_audio.py` script (seed 20261102). Original model
and atlas licensing is documented separately in `RunnerGame/CREDITS.md`.

The included Freesound recordings are CC0 and can be copied, adapted and
redistributed without attribution. Their linked source pages record each
creator and the license. These files are used as sound effects, not for model
training.
