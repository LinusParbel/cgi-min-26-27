# Modular urban street

Original generic European street, metres, common origin at road centre at ground height. Five parts: 7.5 m asphalt road and two 2 m pavements including 0.18 m curbs. Length 20 m, endpoints Y +/-10, pavement top Z .15. Source Blender Z-up/Y longitudinal; export Y-up/-Z longitudinal. All pivots zero. Repeat by translating 20 m longitudinally.

Restrained charcoal asphalt, pale granite curbs and warm grey rectangular concrete paving. Two lane divider markings at X +/-1.25 m define three 2.5 m lanes and are procedural, 3 m dash / 2 m gap. Paving and curb joint patterns have integral repeats over 20 m. No logos, external images or photo pixels. One opaque 2048 atlas; <=3000 triangles. Candidate remains in Art/out pending review.
