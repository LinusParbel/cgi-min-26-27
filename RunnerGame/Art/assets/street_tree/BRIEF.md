# Generic street tree

Original deterministic deciduous tree, authored proportions rather than a particular species or real object. Bent tapering trunk and asymmetric branching support a broad irregular crown. Two thousand small opaque diamond leaves have explicit reverse faces, no alpha cards or ellipsoid foliage. Dimensions and seed in spec.json; source Z up in metres, ground-centred trunk origin. No external assets. Candidate only; no game installation.
