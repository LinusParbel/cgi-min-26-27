### Hannover pedestrian route blockout (candidate, 2026-10-23)

**Imported building geometry:** “Digitales 3D-Stadtmodell Hannover, LoD2”, Landeshauptstadt Hannover, Fachbereich Planen und Stadtentwicklung, Bereich Geoinformation. CC BY 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source: https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Verwaltungen-Kommunen/Die-Verwaltung-der-Landeshauptstadt-Hannover/Dezernate-und-Fachbereiche-der-LHH/Stadtentwicklung-und-Bauen/Fachbereich-Planen-und-Stadtentwicklung/Geoinformation/Open-GeoData/3D-Stadtmodell-und-Gel%C3%A4ndemodell/Digitales-3D-Stadtmodell . Retrieved 23 October 2026. The publisher's “4.0 DE” link is the German-language deed of the International licence.

Adaptations: selected 504 source building objects for the route area, triangulated surfaces while retaining polygon holes, converted to local metre coordinates, added an original grey swatch and exported for the engine. In the separate playable blockout copy, each building's minimum GroundSurface elevation is normalised to a flat plane; original east/north coordinates and heights relative to its base are retained. Absolute roof elevations are not preserved in that copy. Source IDs, original conversion, per-building vertical shifts, hashes and publisher/licence evidence remain in `Art/assets/hannover_route/` and `Art/assets/hannover_pedestrian_blockout/`. Imported city buildings are not represented as manually authored project geometry. No endorsement by the city is implied.

**Aerial reference:** “Digitale Orthophotos 2025 (DOP20)”, same publisher, CC BY 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source: https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Verwaltungen-Kommunen/Die-Verwaltung-der-Landeshauptstadt-Hannover/Dezernate-und-Fachbereiche-der-LHH/Stadtentwicklung-und-Bauen/Fachbereich-Planen-und-Stadtentwicklung/Geoinformation/Open-GeoData/Digitale-Orthophotos-DOP20/Digitale-Orthophotos-2025 . Survey 6 March 2025; publication 20 January 2026; retrieved 23 October 2026. Changes to reference images: selected tiles, cropped/composited, resampled from 20 cm ground resolution to a 50 cm/pixel overview, grid/labels and proposal overlay. Imagery is reference-only; no aerial pixels are used in runtime textures. Tile hashes, ZIP CRC checks and georeferencing are in `Art/assets/hannover_route_reference/overview-georef.json` and `.model-research/hannover_route_reference/`.

**Project-authored blockout:** Route choices, paving, simplified openings/walls/guardrails and procedural paving texture are authored with AI-assisted scripts. Promenade depth (3 m) and flat playable height are explicit prototype assumptions, not survey data. The original north-west path proposal was rejected by clearance checks; the candidate uses the south-east upper corridor without shrinking the supplied footprints/openings. Existing clock, scooter, rider and props retain their separate credits above. No Google imagery/meshes or OpenStreetMap geometry is incorporated in this blockout.

Professor-facing method and limitations: `docs/art/HANNOVER_MAP_SOURCES_AND_METHOD.md`. This attribution must accompany the exported derivative map. The city-data licence does not replace the licences of other assets or the project as a whole.


### Public-realm revision and street-level architecture (24 October 2026)

User-provided annotated aerial screenshots were inspected to identify the intended area, not traced into shipped geometry or used as texture pixels. Their images are not redistributed with this project. Ground and opening measurements use the official licensed aerial reference and CityGML. The v2 model covers the full public spaces instead of the earlier narrow track; opening lip interpretation and route were revised, with older proposals retained as negative evidence.

Kröpcke facade overlays are source-aligned photo-informed architectural interpretations: the Center envelope is updated from the older official model toward the 2014 six-storey appearance; the Magis rounded skin projects about2m beyond its old source parcel on visible sides. Hidden returns and current storefront details remain unverified. No historic brand logos or photographic texture pixels are included. Actual facade mesh and tree-canopy clearances were independently checked.
