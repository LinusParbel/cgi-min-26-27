# Van crash implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement inline.

**Goal:** One funny terminal van-hit scene with collapsing road and timed sound.
**Architecture:** Simulation owns a frozen contact snapshot and six-second clock.
The street renderer consumes it for actor poses, camera and split-road geometry.
A scene audio player follows the same clock, including pause and reset.
**Tech Stack:** Existing C#/OpenTK, Blender rig authoring, PCM WAV/Windows waveOut.
**Spec:** [animation.md](../../../RunnerGame/Assets/Animations/VanCrash/animation.md)

## Global constraints

Use the isolated worktree. Preserve other obstacle rules and pedestrian maps.
Reference shared assets; new exports and sound belong to VanCrash. No commits.

## Review focus

- Van contact during immunity must still end the run.
- Pause/focus loss must freeze clock and sound without automatic resume.
- Restart at any beat must clear pose, camera, hole and sound.
- Airborne/lateral collision must not teleport the rider at impact.
- Preview must exercise real collision rather than a separate animation path.
